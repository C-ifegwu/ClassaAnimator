# Director's Cut

Interactive Character Experience System
Theme: Virtual Performance / Presentation

About the Project:
Director's Cut is an interactive project where you play as a film director auditioning three actors on a movie set. The actors are The Boss, Remy, and Peasant Girl.

You can direct them using the buttons on the screen or with your keyboard. When you trigger an action, the character performs their animation on the stage, speaks a voice line or makes footstep sounds, and the director display updates to show who is performing and what they are doing.

Controls:

Keyboard:
- W: Walk in place (with footstep sounds)
- Spacebar: Talk (plays voice line)
- 1: Reaction 1
- 2: Celebrate (Remy dances, The Boss waves, Peasant Girl points)
- 3: Special action (The Boss head nod)
- Tab: Switch between the three actors

UI Buttons:
- Actor Selection: Choose between The Boss, Remy, and Peasant Girl to put them on the stage mark.
- Action Buttons: Walk, Talk, React 1, Celebrate, and Special.
- Navigation: Buttons to enter the Director Studio, open Settings, and go Back.
- Audio Settings: Sliders for Master, Music, and Sound Effects volume, plus a Mute button.

Screens:
- Main Menu: Title screen where you can launch the studio or open settings.
- Director Studio: The main screen where you control the actors, see their current status, and view the director history log.
- Settings: Where you can adjust volume sliders or mute all sound.

Assets Used:
- Character models and animations from Mixamo
- Menu UI buttons and panels from SlimUI Modern Menu
- Sound effects from Classic Footstep SFX and Urok the Orc Voice Pack
- UI animations made with DOTween
